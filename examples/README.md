# Worked example: Jamie Rivera (fictional)

Jamie Rivera does not exist. Neither do BrightCart, Example Corp, or
anyone named in this folder. This example exists so you can see a
complete, working configuration before you write your own:

- `jamie-rivera/profile/preferences.json` — a filled preferences file.
- `jamie-rivera/profile/candidate.md` — a filled candidate profile.
- `jamie-rivera/companies.jsonl`, `contacts.jsonl`,
  `applications/submitted.jsonl`, `outreach/steps.jsonl` — one small
  pipeline, mid-cadence: Jamie applied to two jobs on Mon Oct 5, 2026,
  Day 1 touches are sent, Day 3 follow-ups are drafted and waiting for
  approval, and one step shows the auto-skip rule (a contact with no
  verified email gets no email steps).

Jamie's situation, in one paragraph: based in Denver, CO; six years in
retail, most recently a shift lead; taught himself front-end
development and now builds and maintains the website for a friend's
small business; some community-college coursework, no degree; exactly
one professional reference (his former store manager). His filter:
$55k+ salary or $26+/hr, within 15 miles of home or remote, no roles
requiring a finished degree, no named-references-up-front forms, and
nothing that collides with his Tuesday/Thursday evening classes.

Copy the shapes, not the facts. Your file should read like your life,
not Jamie's.
